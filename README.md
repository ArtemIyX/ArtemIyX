## About Me

I'm an **Unreal Engine / C++ Engineer** focused primarily on the **client side of games and interactive applications**.

My work is more than just gameplay features. I work on improving runtime performance, debugging production issues, building subsystems, integrating network-backed functionality, representing server state on the client, creating reusable plugins, and solving architectural problems in large Unreal Engine projects.

I have been working on **Endless War: Legacy**, an MMO RPG targeting the China market.

My strongest area is Unreal Engine and C++, but I also build **desktop applications, internal tools, backend-connected clients, web-powered interfaces, automation, development utilities, and experimental AI-assisted software**.

I especially enjoy projects where engine code, networking, UI, performance, backend communication, serialization, tooling, and developer experience intersect.

> [!NOTE]
> The process of making the system of the product is as exciting to me as the product itself.

## AI-Assisted Development / Vibe Coding

I actively experiment with **AI-assisted development** as an engineering workflow rather than treating AI only as code autocomplete.

I maintain a **vibe-code project** and a **vibe-code instrument / workflow** to explore different levels of AI involvement in real development.

- **AI-oriented** — implementation, iteration, refactoring, docs, investigation
- **Semi-AI** — developer-led architecture with AI-assisted execution
- **AI-assisted traditional** — research, review, debugging, tests, boilerplate
- **Rapid prototyping** — validate ideas before committing to architecture
- **AI-supported exploration** — compare APIs, libraries, and approaches

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The key question for me is not **“AI or no AI?”** but **“what level of automation is appropriate for this task?”**

Human control remains especially important for architecture, requirements, debugging, review, performance, security, maintainability, and understanding the system being built.

## Performance & Large-Scale Simulation

Performance work is one of the areas I enjoy most in Unreal Engine.

A notable example from my experience is handling **10,000+ animated entities at around 60 FPS**, which required careful decisions around representation, update cost, architecture, and per-frame work.

- ECS-style architecture / Unreal MASS
- Large-scale entity representation
- Update frequency reduction
- Profiling-driven optimization
- Multithreading where appropriate
- Reduced UObject / Actor overhead
- Simulation / presentation separation
- Lightweight representations when possible

> [!TIP]
> I prefer optimization based on profiling and measurable bottlenecks rather than assumptions.
